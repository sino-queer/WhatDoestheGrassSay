# 野草说 What Does the Grass Say

嫩芽冲出种皮，阳光穿透阴云，野草顶开石缝。

野草说是 Sinoqueer Sverige 发起的版画工作坊系列，邀请酷儿社群成员一起动手刻版、印刷，把彼此的经历、情绪与想象留在纸上。每一期工作坊都有一个共同的主题，参与者围绕它自由创作——没有标准答案，只有一次次真实的表达。

这个网站是这些作品的数字展厅：按期数与主题分类浏览，中英双语呈现每一件作品的标题、作者与阐释。

A sprout splits open its seed coat. Sunlight slips through heavy clouds. Wild grass finds its way through cracks in concrete.

*What Does the Grass Say* is an ongoing printmaking workshop series by Sinoqueer Sverige, where members of the queer community carve and print together — turning shared experience, emotion, and imagination into ink on paper. Each session centers on a theme; there are no right answers, only honest expression.

This site is the digital home for that growing body of work: browse by session and theme, with every piece presented bilingually — title, artist, and interpretation.
